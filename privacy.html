Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • 3D Grand Championship Edition

1. Overview

Chess Royale provides a grandmaster-tier 3D chess simulation that operates entirely client-side on your device. We hold player privacy as a highest principle.

Offline & Client-Side Execution: All chess engines (Stockfish WASM/Web Worker & Minimax algorithms) and Three.js 3D renderings execute purely in your local browser sandbox. No match moves or gameplay sessions are uploaded to any remote server.

2. Data Saved on Your Device

To enable persistence across browser visits, the application stores data locally via localStorage:

  • Player Preferences: Sound effect toggles, board perspective (White/Black), camera view choices, and theme selections.
  • Match Records: ELO ratings, match results, and locally recorded move notations (PGN/FEN).
  • Engine Configurations: Selected Stockfish search depth and difficulty modes.

3. Third-Party CDNs & Assets

To deliver optimized graphics and typography, the application references:

  • Google Fonts: For typography rendering.
  • Three.js & Stockfish.js: Standard open-source graphics and chess engine libraries loaded directly or bundled locally.

We do not operate third-party tracking beacons, cross-site identity cookies, or third-party ad networks.

4. Children's Privacy

Chess Royale is suitable for players and chess enthusiasts of all ages. We do not gather or store any personal information from children or adults alike.

5. Clearing Your Data

You can completely clear your chess stats, win records, and preferences at any time by clearing site data or cookies for this domain in your browser settings.
